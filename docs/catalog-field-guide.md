# OpenKPIs Catalog Field Guide

This guide explains the information shown on OpenKPIs KPI, Metric, Dimension, and Event records. Use the tab for the type you are creating or reviewing. Each definition states the field's purpose, the value to enter, its format, and examples.

The same contract drives the OpenKPIs editor, validation, AI-assisted writing, GitHub content, and public catalog. Contract version: **5**.

## KPI fields

A KPI is a decision-oriented performance indicator interpreted against an objective or business outcome.

### Identity and definition

| Field | What it means and what to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Catalog Type**<br>`entity_type` | Identifies the kind of catalog concept: KPI for a decision-oriented performance indicator, Metric for a reusable quantitative measure, Dimension for a descriptive breakdown attribute, or Event for one observable business occurrence.<br>**Enter:** System field that selects the KPI, Metric, Dimension, or Event schema.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum. Choose from: kpi; metric; dimension; event<br>**Good example:** kpi<br>**Avoid:** KPI record |
| **URL Slug**<br>`slug` | Provides the stable URL and catalog identity for the concept.<br>**Enter:** Use a stable lowercase URL identity with words separated by hyphens.<br>**Rules:** Format: lowercase-kebab-case. Length: 3–120 characters<br>**Good example:** abandoned-cart-recovery-rate<br>**Avoid:** Abandoned Cart Recovery Rate KPI |
| **Name**<br>`name` | States the prevailing business or analytics term a general reader will recognize.<br>**Enter:** Use the established business term only; do not add a definition, platform, or implementation note.<br>**Rules:** Format: plain-text. Length: 3–100 characters<br>**Good example:** Abandoned Cart Recovery Rate<br>**Avoid:** Advanced Seven-Day GA4 Cart Cohort Recovery KPI |
| **Also Known As**<br>`aliases` | Lists established alternate names or abbreviations for the same concept.<br>**Enter:** Optionally add established equivalent names or abbreviations, separated by semicolons. Do not add related concepts or implementation names.<br>**Rules:** Format: semicolon-list<br>**Good example:** ATC Rate; Add-to-Cart Rate<br>**Avoid:** purchase; checkout; useful conversion measure |
| **Description**<br>`description` | Explains what the record means and why a business reader would use it.<br>**Enter:** Define what is measured or captured and why it matters in 1–2 plain-language sentences.<br>**Rules:** Format: one-or-two-sentences. Length: 40–220 characters<br>**Good example:** Percentage of abandoned carts that later result in a completed order. It shows how much abandoned shopping demand returns to purchase.<br>**Avoid:** A sophisticated matured-cart cohort metric computed from identity-stitched event streams. |
| **Formula**<br>`formula` | Shows the shortest unambiguous arithmetic or transformation that produces the value.<br>**Enter:** Write one simple business expression. Put timing, exclusions, deduplication, and attribution in Calculation Notes.<br>**Rules:** Format: business-expression. Length: 5–300 characters<br>**Good example:** (Orders from previously abandoned carts / Total abandoned carts) × 100<br>**Avoid:** Recovered matured cohorts divided by eligible canonicalized cart entities after stitching. |

### Classification and ownership

| Field | What it means and what to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Category**<br>`category` | Classifies the concept by its primary measurement purpose.<br>**Enter:** Choose one primary measurement purpose. Classify Dimensions by the analysis domain they represent. Use Satisfaction only for direct perception measures, Operational Efficiency for business-process execution or operational Dimensions, Technical Reliability for system behavior, and Data Quality & Governance for identity, consent, quality, control, or compliance.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum. Choose from: Acquisition; Engagement; Conversion; Revenue & Monetization; Retention; Satisfaction; Operational Efficiency; Technical Reliability; Data Quality & Governance; Other<br>**Good example:** Conversion for Purchase Completed; Conversion for Purchase Product Identifier; Revenue & Monetization for Average Items per Order; Operational Efficiency for Order Fulfillment Time; Operational Efficiency for Fulfillment Channel; Technical Reliability for Page Load Time<br>**Avoid:** Experience; Operations; Performance; Revenue and Conversion |
| **Tags**<br>`tags` | Adds concise search terms that help readers discover the record.<br>**Enter:** Add 2–8 short search labels for the domain, journey, or subject; press Enter after each tag.<br>**Rules:** Format: string-list<br>**Good example:** cart recovery; checkout; conversion<br>**Avoid:** This KPI is used by e-commerce teams to understand cart recovery |
| **Industry**<br>`industry` | Identifies every industry where the definition genuinely applies without modification.<br>**Enter:** Select every industry where the definition genuinely applies. E-commerce/Retail is a generation priority, not a site restriction.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum-list. Choose from: Retail; E-commerce; SaaS; Healthcare; Education; Finance; Media; Technology; Manufacturing; Travel & Hospitality; Telecommunications; Automotive; Other<br>**Good example:** E-commerce; Retail; Telecommunications<br>**Avoid:** E-commerce only because the current automation batch focuses there |
| **Business Criticality**<br>`business_criticality` | Expresses the materiality of missing or incorrect measurement to business decisions.<br>**Enter:** Choose the decision risk if this measurement is missing or wrong; do not rate the current result.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum. Choose from: Critical; High; Medium; Low<br>**Good example:** High<br>**Avoid:** Critical because this month's result declined |
| **Journey / Navigation Area**<br>`core_area` | Identifies one to three journey or navigation areas where the concept occurs.<br>**Enter:** Select 1–3 journey or navigation areas, such as Cart, Checkout, or Store Experience. Choose Owning Function separately.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum-list. Choose from: Acquisition; Discovery; Consideration; Product Experience; Cart; Checkout; Payment; Purchase; Post-Purchase; Fulfillment; Returns & Refunds; Loyalty & Retention; Customer Service; Account & Identity; Store Experience; Cross-Journey<br>**Good example:** Cart; Checkout; Store Experience; Purchase<br>**Avoid:** Store Operations; Digital Analytics |
| **Owning Function**<br>`owner` | Identifies the operating function accountable for the definition, quality, and interpretation.<br>**Enter:** Choose the operating function accountable for this definition and its measurement quality.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum. Choose from: Marketing; Merchandising; Product Management; Digital Experience; Customer Experience; Customer Support; Store Operations; E-commerce Operations; Order Management; Fulfillment & Logistics; Inventory; Payments; Returns; Loyalty & CRM; Finance; Risk & Compliance; Data & Technology; People Operations<br>**Good example:** E-commerce Operations<br>**Avoid:** Jane Doe; Checkout; GA4 team |
| **Scope**<br>`scope` | Identifies the closest standard measurement level at which the value is associated or the event is captured.<br>**Enter:** Choose User, Session, Event, or Product. A purchase or order is Event scope; an item is Product scope. Put the precise business grain in the definition and formula.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum. Choose from: User; Session; Event; Product<br>**Good example:** User; Session; Event; Product<br>**Avoid:** Purchase; Order; Cart; Global |

### Measurement and governance

| Field | What it means and what to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Measurement Channels**<br>`measurement_channels` | Identifies the environments where the behavior or business fact occurs and is observed.<br>**Enter:** Select where the measured action or fact occurs. Use Report Attributes for paid, organic, referral, affiliate, or social acquisition classifications.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum-list. Choose from: Web; Mobile App; Backend / API; Store / POS; Marketing Messaging; Call Center / Support; External Platform / Partner; Connected Device / IoT; Other Offline<br>**Good example:** Web; Mobile App; Store / POS<br>**Avoid:** Paid Search; Executive Dashboard |
| **Source Data**<br>`source_data` | Lists the source-system families that supply the required facts without turning the field into architecture prose.<br>**Enter:** Select 1–5 source families. Add Offline Operational System for non-browser collection; combine it with Digital Analytics only for a hybrid implementation.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum-list. Choose from: Digital Analytics; Commerce Platform; Order Management; Product Information; Point of Sale; Inventory or Warehouse; CRM or CDP; Marketing Platform; Customer Support; Payment Processor; Finance or ERP; Experimentation Platform; Survey or Voice of Customer; Application or Server Logs; Offline Operational System; External Partner Data. Length: 2–160 characters<br>**Good example:** Digital Analytics; Commerce Platform; Order Management; Point of Sale; Order Management; Offline Operational System<br>**Avoid:** Best-in-class omnichannel enterprise data platform that combines every customer source |
| **Dependencies**<br>`dependencies` | Links the existing catalog records that are required to calculate or implement this record.<br>**Enter:** Add existing prerequisite slugs under Events, Metrics, Dimensions, or KPIs. This is a reference map, not a sentence or enum.<br>**Rules:** Format: typed-slug-map<br>**Good example:** {"Events":["add-to-cart","purchase"],"Metrics":[],"Dimensions":[],"KPIs":[]}<br>**Avoid:** Needs GA4 configured correctly and a useful dashboard |
| **Report Attributes**<br>`report_attributes` | Lists valid dimensions readers can use to filter, group, or compare the value.<br>**Enter:** List 1–8 available breakdown dimensions, one per line, such as device category, market, or customer type.<br>**Rules:** Format: line-list<br>**Good example:** Device category Market Customer type<br>**Avoid:** Revenue, conversion rate, and every useful field |
| **Recommended Reporting Views**<br>`dashboard_usage` | Recommends a small set of reporting views where the measure would help a business user make a decision.<br>**Enter:** Optionally enter up to 3 short recommended reporting views, such as Checkout Funnel or Cart Recovery. Do not claim that a dashboard is deployed.<br>**Rules:** Format: semicolon-list<br>**Good example:** Checkout Funnel; Cart Recovery; Lifecycle Marketing<br>**Avoid:** Executive Dashboard; Acme Global Commerce Command Center |
| **Segment Eligibility**<br>`segment_eligibility` | Defines the business-readable condition under which a record qualifies for a segment or audience.<br>**Enter:** Write one audience condition, such as Cart Status EQUALS “Abandoned” AND Cart Value GREATER THAN 0. Quote text values and use ALL-CAPS operators; do not write SQL or Yes/No, and do not add reporting dates, placeholders, or explanations.<br>**Rules:** Format: business-condition-expression. Length: 8–220 characters<br>**Good example:** Cart Status EQUALS "Abandoned" AND Cart Value GREATER THAN 0; Purchase Product Identifier EXISTS; Fulfillment Channel EQUALS "Store pickup"<br>**Avoid:** true; Yes — this can be segmented; Checkout Start Date IN Selected Reporting Period; Location ID EQUALS Configured Report Location ID; Fulfillment Channel EQUALS "Store pickup" (illustrative example) |
| **Data Sensitivity**<br>`data_sensitivity` | Sets the handling level required by the most sensitive input or output.<br>**Enter:** Choose the handling level required by the most sensitive collected field or reported output.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum. Choose from: Public; Internal; Restricted<br>**Good example:** Internal<br>**Avoid:** Restricted because all analytics data is private |
| **Contains PII**<br>`pii_flag` | Indicates whether the implementation collects or processes personally identifiable information.<br>**Enter:** Select only when the implementation collects or processes personally identifiable information.<br>**Rules:** Format: boolean<br>**Good example:** false when only anonymous cart and transaction identifiers are used<br>**Avoid:** true because every analytics identifier is automatically PII |
| **Aggregation / Lookback Window**<br>`aggregation_window` | Lists supported reporting windows for KPI/Metric records and optional lookback windows for Dimensions.<br>**Enter:** Choose supported reporting windows. For a Dimension, use this only as a Lookback Window when prior behavior determines the value.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum-list. Choose from: Event; Session; User; Selected Reporting Period; Hourly; Daily; Weekly; Monthly; Quarterly; Yearly; Rolling 7 Days; Rolling 30 Days; Rolling 90 Days; Configured Lookback<br>**Good example:** Daily; Weekly; Monthly<br>**Avoid:** Every option because the dashboard can change dates |
| **Measure Type**<br>`measure_type` | Classifies the mathematical form of the reported value.<br>**Enter:** Choose the mathematical type that matches the formula and displayed unit.<br>**Rules:** Format: enum. Choose from: Counter; Rate; Ratio; Percentage; Average; Sum; Currency<br>**Good example:** Percentage<br>**Avoid:** Rate for a formula multiplied by 100 and displayed as a percent |
| **Calculation Notes**<br>`calculation_notes` | Records only the rules needed to calculate or interpret the value consistently.<br>**Enter:** Write 1–5 short numbered rules. Cover only necessary timing, exclusions, deduplication, attribution, and empty-denominator handling; omit filler.<br>**Rules:** Format: emoji-numbered-bullets<br>**Good example:** 1️⃣ Count each confirmed order once. 2️⃣ Use the configured recovery period. 3️⃣ Return null when no carts qualify.<br>**Avoid:** A seven-bullet technical design containing implementation history, schema caveats, and review commentary |
| **Business Use Case**<br>`business_use_case` | Explains who uses the record, what signal prompts attention, and what decision follows.<br>**Enter:** Write 1–3 short numbered uses. Each should name who acts and the decision the information supports.<br>**Rules:** Format: emoji-numbered-bullets<br>**Good example:** 1️⃣ Checkout teams investigate a sustained decline. 2️⃣ CRM teams compare recovery methods before changing outreach.<br>**Avoid:** Track the KPI because it is important and useful for the business |

### Platform implementation

| Field | What it means and what to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **GA4 Event**<br>`ga4_event` | Lists the GA4 events required to create the record's data.<br>**Enter:** List required GA4 events, one per line as “event_name — short purpose”. Do not write tagging or tracking-plan instructions.<br>**Rules:** Format: event-inventory. Length: 0–800 characters<br>**Good example:** add_to_cart — establishes cart contents purchase — confirms the completed order<br>**Avoid:** Collect purchase and configure tags, mappings, identifiers, dimensions, and reporting rules. |
| **Adobe Event**<br>`adobe_event` | Lists the Adobe collection events or actions required to create the record's data.<br>**Enter:** List required Adobe events/actions, one per line as “event_name — short purpose”. Keep mapping instructions in the mapping fields.<br>**Rules:** Format: event-inventory. Length: 0–800 characters<br>**Good example:** cart_add — establishes cart contents purchase — confirms the completed order<br>**Avoid:** Map additions and purchases, configure rules, populate XDM, and replace the tenant namespace. |
| **W3 Data Layer**<br>`w3_data_layer` | Shows the canonical business-event payloads from which the platform-specific mappings are derived.<br>**Enter:** Use digitalData.event with one entry per canonical Event dependency. Include only business objects and identifiers used by the other mappings or queries.<br>**Rules:** Format: ceddl-json<br>**Good example:** {"digitalData":{"event":[{"eventInfo":{"eventName":"purchase_completed"}}]}}<br>**Avoid:** window.digitalData = {...} or a purchase-only fragment when several lifecycle events are required |
| **GA4 Data Layer**<br>`ga4_data_layer` | Shows the exact GA4 dataLayer payloads needed to produce every listed GA4 event.<br>**Enter:** Provide a JSON array with one payload per GA4 event. Names and parameters must match GA4 Event, Event Parameters, and BigQuery SQL.<br>**Rules:** Format: ga4-payload-set<br>**Good example:** [{"event":"add_to_cart","ecommerce":{"items":[]}},{"event":"purchase","ecommerce":{"transaction_id":"T-123","items":[]}}]<br>**Avoid:** A purchase-only payload when add_to_cart and begin_checkout are also listed |
| **Adobe Client Data Layer**<br>`adobe_client_data_layer` | Shows the Adobe Client Data Layer pushes needed for every listed Adobe event.<br>**Enter:** Provide a JSON array of Adobe Client Data Layer event objects covering every event named above.<br>**Rules:** Format: adobe-payload-set<br>**Good example:** [{"event":"cart_add","eventInfo":{"cartId":"C-123"}},{"event":"purchase","eventInfo":{"transactionId":"T-123"}}]<br>**Avoid:** One purchase object when the Adobe event inventory also names cart_add |
| **XDM Mapping**<br>`xdm_mapping` | Maps every Adobe event payload into an equivalent ExperienceEvent shape used by AEP Query Service.<br>**Enter:** Provide one ExperienceEvent object per Adobe event. Event types, paths, and parameters must match the Adobe layer and AEP SQL.<br>**Rules:** Format: xdm-payload-set<br>**Good example:** [{"_id":"purchase-T-123","timestamp":"2026-10-16T12:00:00Z","eventType":"commerce.purchases","commerce":{"purchases":{"value":1}}}]<br>**Avoid:** Deprecated endUserIDs or invented _experience.analytics.custom1 paths |
| **GA4 BigQuery SQL**<br>`ga4_sql` | Implements the published definition against the documented GA4 BigQuery export schema.<br>**Enter:** Provide one concise read-only BigQuery query using only the listed GA4 events and mapped parameters. Use timezone-safe _TABLE_SUFFIX pruning and exact event_timestamp bounds. Prefer 25 or fewer executable lines; Event records do not use SQL.<br>**Rules:** Format: read-only-sql<br>**Good example:** SELECT SAFE_DIVIDE(COUNTIF(event_name='purchase'), COUNTIF(event_name='begin_checkout')) * 100 FROM `project.analytics.events_*`<br>**Avoid:** SELECT * plus debug outputs, unrelated joins, or events not declared above |
| **AEP Query Service SQL**<br>`aep_sql` | Implements the same published definition for the selected AEP dataset profile.<br>**Enter:** Provide one concise read-only AEP query using only the listed Adobe events and mapped XDM paths, with the same business result as GA4 SQL. Event records do not use SQL.<br>**Rules:** Format: read-only-sql<br>**Good example:** SELECT SUM(commerce.purchases.value) FROM {{AEP_EXPERIENCE_EVENT_DATASET}} WHERE eventType='commerce.purchases'<br>**Avoid:** A query using an assumed universal table or different business rules from GA4 |

### Catalog connections

| Field | What it means and what to enter |
| --- | --- |
| **Related KPIs**<br>`related_kpis` | Links existing KPIs that help readers compare or interpret this KPI.<br>**Enter:** Enter existing KPI slugs that aid comparison, separated by semicolons. Do not repeat required dependencies.<br>**Rules:** Format: semicolon-slug-list<br>**Good example:** cart-abandonment-rate;cart-to-purchase-rate<br>**Avoid:** purchase;order_id;new recovery KPI to create later |
